# Blind accessibility beta 0.5.1

Fixes found after the first public beta. No game data is included: you need your own USA disc image. Unzip the folder anywhere you can write to, then run LuigisMansionRecomp.exe. If you have 0.5.0, the game offers this update itself.

Tea Room and Cellar:
- The emblems that flip Luigi between the floor and the ceiling are now in the Go to menu, under Furniture. Luigi walks onto one, and you hear where it put him: "Flipped onto the ceiling", "Dropped onto the table" or "Back on the floor". In the Tea Room, this is how you reach the table with the white chest.
- The Tea Room's flying saucers no longer hurt Luigi while "No damage from thrown objects" is on. A few other flying objects of the same kind are covered too.

Hints and doors:
- New and fuller room hints for the Tea Room, the Astral Hall, the Observatory and the Balcony, where Boolossus waits.
- The right stick's status now says how many Boos you have caught, once they are out: "Boos caught 7 of 50. The Balcony wants 20".
- In the Go to menu, the Balcony and Secret Altar doors say they are sealed by the Boos until you have caught 20, or 40, and how many you have. Coming near the Balcony door with too few, you hear that a Boo guards it and sends you back to the Foyer.
- Observatory: the meteor has a beacon and a Go to entry, and Lock on aims the vacuum at it, then the throw at the moon. Mario's star is no longer announced before the moon breaks.

Go to menu:
- A walk where Luigi is caught on something and only shuffles in place now gives up and says "Blocked", instead of pushing forever.

Setup and title screen:
- The setup screen's spoken instructions now explain that some buttons sit side by side, so left and right reach them, and that Browse opens a Windows file window you use with the keyboard: type or paste the path to your disc image and press Enter.
- The title screen's demo no longer repeats its scene descriptions every time it loops.

Windows only for now. The game's own text is spoken for the USA version.

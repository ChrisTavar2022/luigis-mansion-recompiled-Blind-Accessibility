# Blind accessibility beta 0.4.0

This is a beta of the blind accessibility version of Luigi's Mansion Recompiled. No game data is included: you need your own USA disc image. Unzip the folder anywhere you can write to, then run LuigisMansionRecomp.exe. If you have 0.3.0, the game offers this update itself.

Boss fights:
- Bosses are the first category in the Go to menu, and Luigi walks to the boss and follows it as it moves.
- Lock on to bosses: hold the right trigger in a boss fight and the vacuum aims at the nearest ball, or at the boss once you hold one. Throws are aimed at the boss. Your own right stick always takes over.
- The boss's phases, health and openings are spoken, with a tone while its heart is exposed, and "has recovered" when the opening ends.
- Thrown objects have their own beacon and a Go to category, and the vacuum keeps hold of an object you grab until you throw it with the left trigger.
- Easier boss hits: a thrown object is steered towards the boss.

Ghosts and Boos:
- Every regular ghost has its real name when caught, and ghosts say when they are stunned: "Gold Ghost stunned. Vacuum now."
- A setting makes stuns last longer, twice as long by default.
- Boos: speech and a tone lead to the furniture a Boo hides in, once the room is lit.
- Shivers, the butler, keeps his tone while he paces his hallway and can be walked to from the Go to menu.

Rooms and puzzles:
- Room hints, spoken when they apply to the room as it is now, and a cleared room says so when you enter.
- Candles: unlit candles are listed and spoken, and the Parlor's candles are covered.
- Keys say which door they open. Fake doors are warned about.
- Fire, water and ice: medals, element pickups and the meter are spoken, and medal chests and element spirits have beacons.
- Conservatory: the instruments have their own Go to category and count as you play them, and Melody can be walked to once she appears.
- Gallery portraits are described, and there is a Portraits category in Go to.
- Hidden items, Speedy Spirits and cheese spots are in the Go to menu, as are posters and wall buttons.
- Chests open more reliably: Luigi tries each side of a chest in turn, and stops as soon as something comes out.

Go to menu:
- Changing category says its name and position. Repeated names are numbered, and places you have visited or searched are marked.
- Walks across open room edges carry on into the next room.

Comfort settings, each with its own switch:
- No damage from ghosts and bosses, and from thrown objects, on by default. Hazards, floors and lingering damage can be turned off too.
- Health slowly refills after 5 seconds without being hurt.
- Dropped coins, bills and hearts do not vanish.

Also:
- The Game Boy Horror's item and ghost list (Z) is spoken.
- The Accessibility tab has sub-tabs: General, Gameplay, Damage and Audio cues. The audio cues are one list: A turns a cue on or off, Y plays it.
- Cutscene audio descriptions, and a fix for the opening going silent after the title demo.
- Many fixes from a full bug pass, including the Bowser and King Boo fight and starting a new game in the same session.

Windows only for now. The game's own text is spoken for the USA version.

# Blind accessibility beta 0.3.0

This is a beta of the blind accessibility version of Luigi's Mansion Recompiled. No game data is included: you need your own USA disc image. Unzip the folder anywhere you can write to, then run LuigisMansionRecomp.exe.

- Pathfinding now uses a layout of the whole map, every floor and staircase, so long hallways and big rooms work. Luigi goes around chests and Toads, and if he cannot get right up to something he goes as close as he can and says so.
- Right stick status: each press says the next of health, total money, and room and floor. After 5 seconds it starts from health again. In menus, cutscenes and conversations it repeats the last announcement.
- Furniture: a new Furniture category in the Go to menu, and pressing A says what Luigi is facing.
- Game Boy Horror: the map (Y) says the floor, your room and the rooms you have visited. The search view (X) names what is in the middle of the view as you look around, and says when to look up or down.
- Each audio cue can be turned on or off in the Accessibility tab.
- Rooms, floors, doors and ghost captures are spoken.

Windows only for now. The game's own text is spoken for the USA version.

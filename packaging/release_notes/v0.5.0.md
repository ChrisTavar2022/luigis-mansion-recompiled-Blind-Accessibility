# Blind accessibility beta 0.5.0

The first public beta of the blind accessibility version of Luigi's Mansion Recompiled, released on the game's 25th anniversary. No game data is included: you need your own USA disc image. Unzip the folder anywhere you can write to, then run LuigisMansionRecomp.exe. If you have 0.4.0, the game offers this update itself.

Doors, keys and finding your way:
- Walking to a door with the Go to menu now works for doors that open with no door scene, such as the Rec Room's door to its hallway: Luigi walks through and you hear "Through the door".
- The left shoulder button, in a hallway, reminds you which locked doors your keys open and on which floor, such as "Your key opens the Courtyard, on the first floor." With no unused keys, it says so.
- The stairwell by the Rec Room explains that the Tea Room door at the top is on fire, and where to get water to put it out.
- Burning doors are in Go to under Elements, and spraying water aims at plants, the sprout and burning doors in front of you.

Boss and mini boss help:
- Biff Atlas, in the Rec Room: "Hit 1 of 3" for each punching bag hit, and a warning that he swings back.
- Bogmire: speech when a shadow hits him, and his shadows drift into the running vacuum (setting).
- Mr. Luggs: his fight spoken step by step, with his food in Go to.
- Portrait ghosts and Melody have the boss tone and are listed under Bosses.
- Balls drift into the running vacuum in boss fights and with Nana's yarn and Slim's billiard balls, so a dropped ball is easier to pick up again (setting).
- Shivers, the butler, stays silent until you have visited the Butler's Room, or visited and left the Laundry Room, so you hear about him when he matters.

Sounds:
- Recorded sounds for doors, rocking horses, keys, coins, ball bounces and moving floors, from Kenney's CC0 sound packs.
- Each audio cue has its own volume, in Accessibility, Audio cues (left and right change it).
- Both shoulder buttons together turn every audio cue off, or back on.
- Chauncey's rocking horses have their own beacon, and Mario's items have their own cue.
- Vibration when the vacuum hooks a ghost or a Boo (setting).

Also:
- Every ghost says when it is stunned.
- Objects in a room are heard as soon as you arrive, and coins on the floor are found by room.
- The search view turns to cheese, mouse holes, the telescope, the crystal ball, mirrors and dog houses; mirrors say they lead back to the Foyer.
- Courtyard: the well is in Go to with what opens it, Sparks is named and warned about, and rooms say how many ghosts are left.
- The Master Bedroom curtains and the punching bags are in the Go to menu, and the Storage Room's wall button is named.
- More room hints.

Windows only for now. The game's own text is spoken for the USA version.

# Guide notes

What the walkthroughs say that matters for the mod, in our own words, from IGN's Luigi's Mansion guide (read in Chrome on 2026-11-01). Source: https://www.ign.com/wikis/luigis-mansion/Area_1 and the Area 2, 3 and 4 pages. Facts here are unconfirmed against the game until tested. The room by room steps are in `kRoomHints` in `src/recomp/access_game.cpp`.

## Controls the guide confirms

- Letting go of the right trigger throws what the vacuum holds, and spiked bombs need Luigi to walk up to them, since they are too heavy to pull. The mod's vacuum lock lets go on the left trigger, which gives the same throw.
- Mirrors, cheese, the telescope, mouse holes and doghouse are used with A in the search view (X).

## Things a blind player cannot find without help

- Fake doors: they hurt Luigi. Only the map shows which doors are real. The mod warns near types 225 to 227.
- Cheese: hidden in the search view in several rooms, and it releases a gold mouse. Study (behind the desk), Fortune-teller's Room (behind her chair), Dining Room (behind a chair), Tea Room (back of the room), Safari Room (left of the chaise). In Hidden items, from the map's `schrat` generators; the spot is the generator's, near the cheese.
- Emblems in the Tea Room and Cellar swap Luigi between floor and ceiling. Not in the mod.
- Element sources: fire at the Mirror Room coals, water at the kitchen sink and above Washroom 2's toilet, ice at the Tea Room's bucket and the Ceramics Studio's frozen vase. The spirits are in the Go to menu under Elements while they show.
- The sprout in the Boneyard wants water after each boss, and gives a gold diamond at the end.
- Potted plants on the Balcony and in the Courtyard give treasure when watered.
- Mario's five items: hat in the washing machine, letter in the birdhouse, star in the Observatory, shoe from the Twins' chest, glove from the Projection Room.
- Hearts: a Nursery dresser gives a full heal.

## Enemies worth a word

- Sparks explode if vacuumed. Spray them with fire or water, or keep clear.
- Bowling ghosts lob balls. Bowling balls may want adding to the thrown list.
- Boos hide in furniture in lit rooms, and need chasing into the next room when they flee. The mod speaks this from the game's own Boo records, with a tone in place of the radar. The Danger poster's button in the Storage Room lets them out.
- Grabbers and Spooky are shaken off with the stick.
- Boo counts: 5 to open the Washroom, 20 for the Balcony, 40 for the final door, 50 for the gold diamond.

## Boss details added

- Chauncey: teddy bears fly off the wall in the Nursery and can be vacuumed. Punch 10 damage.
- Bogmire: let go of the right trigger as a shadow reaches Luigi, and it fades. His long shadow on the ground shows where he is.
- Boolossus: 15 small Boos. Carry him with the vacuum to a unicorn's horn, and later tilt up to reach it.
- Bowser: a held bomb goes off after a while. If he leaps, let go and run. Hide behind the chimneys when he stomps.

## Shivers

- Needs fire on his candle in the north hallway. He runs the long way round to the Butler's Room, so go straight there and wait. He sits, the candle burns him, and he is vacuumed then. Too slow and he returns to the hallway. A chest then gives the Conservatory key.

## Ideas not built

- Emblems in the Go to menu.
- The Boo radar spoken or as a tone.
- Bowling balls, plates and sheet music in the thrown list, once their types are found.
